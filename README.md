## 📌 About the Project

**Namma Santhe Ledger** is an Android-based financial management application developed for small village market vendors and local businesses.

Traditional handwritten bookkeeping often leads to:
- ❌ Loss of udhar records
- ❌ Calculation mistakes
- ❌ Difficulty tracking payments
- ❌ Poor financial summaries

This application provides a **simple, fast, and user-friendly digital solution** to manage daily business transactions directly from a mobile phone.

---

# ✨ Key Features

## 💰 Income & Expense Tracking
Easily record daily income and expenses.

## 🧾 Udhar Management
Track customer credit transactions and pending dues efficiently.

## 👥 Customer Ledger
Maintain separate records for each customer.

## ✅ Payment Status Tracking
Mark payments as paid and automatically update balances.

## 📊 Dashboard & Reports
View total balance, income, expenses, pending dues, and business summaries.

## 🔍 Customer Search
Quickly search customer transaction history.

## 📱 WhatsApp Reminder
Send payment reminder messages directly through WhatsApp.

## 💾 Permanent Offline Storage
Uses Room Database to securely store records permanently.
